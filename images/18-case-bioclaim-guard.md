# Image brief — Ch 18 Case: BioClaim Guard

**Filename:** `images/18-case-bioclaim-guard.jpg`
**Aspect:** 16:9 hero, Kindle-readable.

**Subject:** A medical claim entering on the left as a single line of text, decomposing into a four-cell PICO grid (Population / Intervention / Comparison / Outcome), the four cells then querying a PubMed-style document index, and emitting on the right as a four-state verdict card (Supported teal / Misleading amber / Contradicted red / Insufficient Evidence slate). The four colors must be distinct enough to be readable in monochrome at thumbnail size.

**Mood:** Clinical, calm, structured. Audit-trail visualization rather than emergency-room visualization.

**Negative space:** Top for chapter title; right edge for the four-state legend.

**Notes:** Avoid stethoscopes, white coats, and pill bottles. The visual story is *one fuzzy question becoming four specific ones, each independently checkable.* If a single design element should be most prominent, make it the slate-colored Insufficient Evidence box — that is the architecturally critical distinction the chapter argues for.
