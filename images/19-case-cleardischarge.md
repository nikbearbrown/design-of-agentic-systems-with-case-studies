# Image brief — Ch 19 Case: ClearDischarge

**Filename:** `images/19-case-cleardischarge.jpg`
**Aspect:** 16:9 hero, Kindle-readable.

**Subject:** A discharge summary on the left in dense clinical typography, transforming into a patient-facing action plan on the right with a four-time-slot medication grid (morning / afternoon / evening / bedtime) and a daily-checklist card. Between them, the dual-gate readability check rendered as two parallel filters — Flesch-Kincaid formula (numerical) and DistilBERT classifier (semantic) — with a retry-loop arrow looping back if either fails.

**Mood:** Helpful, calm, accessibility-first. The visual transformation from clinical-register-text to patient-friendly-grid is the teaching image.

**Negative space:** Top for chapter title; bottom for the Grade 6–8 readability target as a small caption.

**Notes:** Avoid hospital-bed visuals. The story is *the discharge folder, made readable.* Color contrast must remain accessible — the orange for take-with-food vs blue for standard dose must be distinguishable in monochrome.
